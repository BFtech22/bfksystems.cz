Battery energy storage

Scope

What we do for storage systems

Commissioning

Putting the system into operation including the battery management system, inverters and protection devices.

Control by spot prices

Charging when electricity is cheap, discharging when it is expensive – with limits that respect the connection agreement.

Integration with photovoltaics

Using surplus production, peak shaving and backup of selected circuits.

Dispatch control and telemetry

Connection to the operator's dispatch centre, RTU, SCADA and remote supervision.

Compliance

Simulations and tests for the storage as a generating module.

Service

Inspections, firmware updates, diagnostics of weak cells and repairs.

Grid code

Compliance is verified in both directions

A storage system both draws power and feeds it back, so the operator wants evidence for charging and for discharging. The classification and the required scope follow the current rules of the relevant distribution operator – which is why we settle this before the equipment is ordered, not after it is installed.

Watch the ratings

The usable capacity in kWh says nothing about the category. What counts is the rated active power of the module at the connection point.

Upgrades

Adding storage to an existing plant

Adding a battery to a plant that is already running is not only a technical job. Depending on the size and the operator it may mean a change to the connection agreement, new protection settings and fresh compliance evidence.

  • review of the existing connection agreement and protection settings
  • proposal of capacity and power that fits the consumption profile
  • wiring, commissioning and control settings
  • documentation for the operator and permanent operation

Tell us the plant size and the consumption profile and we will say what adding storage would require.

Frequently asked questions

Does storage pay off without photovoltaics?

It can, if the site has a suitable tariff and consumption profile – the margin then comes from the price spread during the day. We calculate it on your real consumption data, not on a general assumption.

Will the storage keep the site running during an outage?

Only if it is designed for it. Backup operation needs the right inverter topology and a defined set of backed-up circuits; it is a design decision, not a setting.

Who is responsible for the batteries themselves?

The warranty is with the manufacturer. We commission the system, document faults and handle the claim with the supplier.
