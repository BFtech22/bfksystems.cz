Processors

The enquiry form is operated by Web3Forms, which forwards the message to our mailbox – it therefore acts as a processor. Our e-mail runs on Google Workspace. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the host records standard access logs. We do not pass your data to anyone else and we do not use it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are kept for up to two years, so that we can follow up on an earlier conversation. Project correspondence is kept for the duration of the contract and for the statutory periods that follow.

Cookies

The website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Nothing is stored in your browser beyond what is needed to display the page.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing or portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to info@bfksystems.cz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů).
