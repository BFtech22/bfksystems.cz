What we do

Services

Commercial and industrial PV plants and battery storage across Czechia.

From commissioning through control systems, medium voltage and substations to service. We concentrate on large and technically demanding projects.

  • Commissioning
  • Dispatch control
  • MV and substations
  • Commercial PV
  • Compliance testing
  • Battery storage
  • Service

Commissioning

PV commissioning

We put utility-scale photovoltaic plants into operation – from functional tests of inverters and protection devices to electrical inspection reports and handover documentation.

  • commissioning of inverters, transformers and protection relays
  • settings according to the connection conditions of the grid operator
  • electrical safety inspection and commissioning records
  • handover documentation and training of the operator

Control

Remote plant control and balancing services

We implement control and telemetry systems that let the plant follow the distribution operator, the market, or both.

  • RTU and control system for MV plants (Weidmüller components)
  • connection of the plant to the distribution operator's dispatch centre
  • SCADA, control of heating and charging stations
  • control based on spot market prices
  • participation in balancing services and aggregation

Medium voltage

MV switchgear and substations

We rebuild and extend medium-voltage sections – from a single switchgear panel to a complete kiosk substation.

  • replacement and extension of MV switchgear panels
  • rebuilds of transformer stations including measurement
  • work according to the connection conditions of the grid operator
  • coordination with the operator up to energisation

PV plants

Commercial and utility-scale PV

We deliver large rooftop and ground-mounted plants for industrial clients and investors, including the parts that usually cause delays: grid connection, protection settings and compliance evidence.

  • design, delivery and installation of the plant
  • MV connection, transformer station, measurement
  • control system and remote supervision
  • compliance evidence and permanent operation

On-site testing

Functional tests of the generating module directly at the plant, with recorded waveforms.

Documentation and negotiation

We compile the evidence and handle the exchange with the distribution operator.
