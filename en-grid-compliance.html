Compliance

Grid compliance and RfG testing

Simulation, on-site testing and the paperwork that goes with it.

Every generating module connected to the Czech grid has to prove that it meets the requirements of the European network code RfG and the national grid code. We provide that proof – by simulation, by testing at the plant, or by both.

Scope

What we do for you

Compliance simulation

A model of the plant in DIgSILENT PowerFactory and the studies required by the distribution operator, with a pass / fail assessment.

On-site testing

Functional tests on the finished plant with recorded waveforms – for category B1 there are six requirements that cannot be proven any other way.

Documentation and negotiation

We compile the evidence into the form the operator expects and handle the correspondence until approval.

Battery storage

Storage is a generating module as well. Classification and the way compliance is proven follow the current rules of the relevant operator.

Rules

Categories of generating modules

The scope of verification follows the category of the generating module.

The category is stated in the connection agreement and that document always prevails.

Mind the units: the kWp of the panels is not the figure that determines the category. What counts is the rated active power of the generating module – 698 kWp of panels can be a module with Pn 550 kW.

Key concept

Three routes to proving a requirement

On-site test

Verification on the finished plant and a test report. Some requirements cannot be proven in any other way.

Simulation

A model of the plant and a report with a pass / fail assessment. For category B2 and above the scope of simulation is considerably wider.

Equipment certificate

Issued by a certification body for a specific device. Our test or simulation report is not a certificate; a certificate replaces a test only where the generating module document allows it.

Categories, operators, deadlines and the required documents are described in detail on our dedicated website.

Ptáte se

Frequently asked questions

Do we need testing for a plant below 100 kW?

Modules in categories A1 and A2 are normally proven by documentation and an equipment certificate, not by on-site testing. The connection agreement and the operator's current rules decide.

What do you need from us to start?

The connection agreement, the single-line diagram, inverter and protection data sheets and the protection settings. For testing we also agree a date when the plant can be operated freely for a few hours.

Do you also handle the communication with the distribution operator?

Yes. We prepare the evidence in the required form and resolve comments with the operator until the module is approved.

Does a battery storage system need the same evidence?

Storage is assessed as a generating module too, and the verification covers both directions of power flow – charging and discharging.
